# Safety

monogatari is a tool for reflection, meaning-making, and resilience. It is **not** therapy, it does
**not** provide medical or psychological advice, it does **not** diagnose, and it is **not** an
emergency service. It is meant to stand alongside real-world support and professional care — never
to replace them.

## If you or someone else may be in danger

Please reach out to local emergency services or a crisis line right away.

- **United States:** call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at 988lifeline.org.
- **United Kingdom & Ireland:** call **116 123** (Samaritans).
- **Canada:** call or text **988**.
- **Australia:** call **13 11 14** (Lifeline).
- **Elsewhere:** the International Association for Suicide Prevention lists crisis centers at
  https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/ . For immediate danger, contact local emergency
  services.

The app keeps an up-to-date, regionally aware version of this list reachable from everywhere inside
it, including while writing.

## How safety is built into the product

- **Grounding is always one click away** — including mid-writing, when it matters most.
- **Nothing is forced.** Difficult material can be approached gently, in small pieces, or set aside.
- **You set the pace.** No streaks, no pressure, no guilt.
- **You stay in control of your data.** Everything is local and encrypted; export and delete are
  always available.

## Design responsibility (for contributors)

- Never present the app as a substitute for professional care.
- Keep crisis resources accurate, regional where possible, and reachable from every screen.
- Prefer language that invites over language that instructs. Avoid anything that could shame.
- Treat all user content as deeply sensitive by default. Local-first, encrypted, no exceptions.
- When in doubt, choose the gentler, safer, more private option.
