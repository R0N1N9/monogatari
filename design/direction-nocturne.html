Thirty-one years, lit scene by scene — and slowly re-known.

Scene ISpring 1996 · age 4
May 1996 · Alder St. kitchen

The House on Alder Street

The kitchen was always warm and always loud. I remember the light more than anything — late afternoon coming sideways through the window, turning the dust into something like snow. I did not know yet that a childhood could be a place you carry, not just a time you leave.

Scene IIAutumn 2011 · age 19
Dec 2011 · one lamp, one chair

Leaving

I packed the car in the dark so no one would see me cry. For a long time I told this as a story about escape. Writing it now, I can see it was also a story about grief — that you can need to go and still lose something real in the going. Both were true. I am allowed to hold both.

Scene IIISummer 2023 · age 31
Jul 2023 · the coast, again

Small Mercies

These days the healing is quiet and unglamorous. A morning walk. A friend who texts back. The old story said I was someone things happened to. The truer story, the one I am re-knowing here, is that I am also someone who kept choosing to begin again.
